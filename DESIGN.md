# Design system: Narrari

- Updated: 25-Nov-26
- Direction: Smoked glass pill, Engraved wordmark, Reader window, Glance card menu panel; meetings: a Dictations and Meetings switch, a tabbed meeting page, a dark call notice plate, the meeting pill or a corner chip
- Prototype: `.nb/design/18-Nov-26/pill-options.html` (A), `logo-options-2.html` (2), `windows-options-2.html` (2); `.nb/design/19-Nov-26/menu-panel.html` (3), `pill-motion-2.html` (glass plate, serif), `window-settings-2.html` (settings); `.nb/design/20-Nov-26/pill-halo.html` (3), `rerun.html` (A), `no-sound.html` (notice C, chooser A); `.nb/design/22-Nov-26/meetings-page.html` (A, tabs Summary, Transcript, Speakers), `call-notice.html` (A), `meeting-bar.html` (A and C, chosen in Settings)

## Product
- What: a menu bar app that turns speech into text in whatever app you are typing in, and keeps a short record of it.
- Audience: one person at their own Mac, all day; the pill is seen for seconds many times a day, the window a few minutes at a time.
- Feels like: serious (monochrome, no accent colour; the Colour recorder look is the one exception), written (dictated words set like a printed page), quiet (the pill only says what is happening).
- Not like: the coloured NB lab products (no colour pair, no Dots symbol); stock System Settings (custom forms, not grouped insets).

The pill's sizes and timings live in NarrariKit (`Sources/NarrariKit/Pill/PillGeometry.swift`, `PillMotion.swift`), because its width and motion rules are tested there. - Spacing: 8 pt rhythm; steps used 2, 4, 8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 20, 22, 32, 36.
- Radius: pill 18 (capsule), caption plate 16 (a capsule at one line), both circular corners; corner chip 18 and call notice 16, the same; pill buttons and pill icon buttons 13 (capsules); menu panel 14, its card 10, its rows 7, keycap 4; window 12 (system), drop target 12; list item 7; buttons, fields, popups, nav rows 6; dropdown list 10, its rows 6; icon button label 6; progress and scrubber 2.
- Shadow: the pill's Liquid Glass draws its own; Solid and the macOS 15 glass: the capsule takes `0 6 18` black 22% plus `0 1 2` black 16%, the plate `0 4 16` black 14% plus `0 1 2` black 10%, the glass looks a 0.5 pt inner rim white 30% top, 13% middle, 10% bottom (Smoked draws it on macOS 26 too), Solid a flat 0.5 pt inner edge white 12%, Clear a 1 pt inner rim white 60% top, 14% middle, 34% bottom, Gloss a 1 pt inner edge white 45% top, 12% middle, 20% bottom, never an outer hairline; menu panel takes the system window shadow (prototype `0 14 36` black 18% light, 50% dark). Windows, the dropdown list and the icon button label use the system shadow.
- Motion: level bars follow the microphone live; notices fade out after 2.5 s, the error stays until the next dictation. Durations for fades: 200 ms ease-out (proposal). Menu panel: opens 4 pt down to its place while fading in, 200 ms, curve (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1); closes with a fade, 150 ms, curve (0.4, 0, 1, 1); Return shows the press for 90 ms before acting; Copy holds "Copied" 300 ms, then closes. Icon button label: shows 0.4 s after the pointer rests on the button or keyboard focus reaches it, goes at once when either leaves. A copy button shows "Copied" for 1.5 s; Transcribe again turns its arrow while it runs and shows the check for 1.5 s when the text lands; Clean up again shows the check for 1.5 s too. The reader's Details open and close with a 200 ms ease-out fade while the chevron turns a quarter. A model getting ready from this Mac changes nothing for its first 400 ms; after that its filled radio turns into a 0.3 arc on the ring at 20%, one turn a second, until it is ready. Never animates: list selection, settings swap, the page, a model row changing group. Reduced motion: dots hold still, the getting-ready arc holds still, the Transcribe again arrow holds still, fades become instant, Details open and close at once, the menu panel appears and goes without motion; level bars stay, because they are data.
- Pill motion (springs as SwiftUI `spring(duration:bounce:)`): appears scale 0.86 and 6 pt low to rest, spring 0.42/0.12, fading in 0.16 s; leaves scale 0.9, 0.24 s ease-in. Width spring 0.32/0. Content swap: out 0.10 s ease-in with 3 pt blur, in 0.20 s (0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), 0.04 s after the swap. Shrinking, content leaves first and the shape moves 0.10 s later; growing, the shape moves at once and the new content, held at the leading padding, shows behind a soft edge that ends 6 pt inside the moving trailing edge; content is clipped to its capsule. Release: press to 0.965 in 0.07 s, back on spring 0.3/0, bars fold on spring 0.2/0, first swap 0.12 s later, caption dims in 0.18 s. Dots: opacity wave 0.35 to 1, period 0.95 s, stagger 0.16. Halo: the top line follows the level on the bars' beat; the working light laps once per 1.2 s; done draws the outline in 0.4 s, then fades in 0.25 s; a caption word settles to 66% over 0.5 s once a newer word arrives. Done: the check draws on (macOS 26) or appears while the capsule closes to 36, held 0.7 s. Caption: enters with its first word, scale 0.92 and 6 pt low, spring 0.4/0.1; width spring 0.24/0, only grows within a dictation; new words fade in 0.18 s with 2 pt blur, 0.06 s after the update or once a widening plate is within 3 pt of fitting them, 0.03 s apart in reading order, and a word already shown that the recogniser rewords changes in place; the plate changes height only for a new line; a new line opens on spring 0.36/0 once the words before it are in, the oldest line clear by 0.45 of its travel. Caption leaves: words first in 0.10 s, then the plate lifts 6 pt to 0.94 in 0.18 s when the text landed, or fades in place in 0.2 s. With Reduce Motion every swap, word and the caption is a 0.15 s linear fade, widths and lines jump while the content is hidden, no press, lift or blur; Halo keeps its top line (data, like the bars), holds a steady outline at 40% while working, lights the whole outline when done and fades it, and settles words in 0.15 s.
- Meeting motion (proposal; the prototypes are still): the meeting pill and the chip appear and leave like the recorder, the chip coming down 6 pt to rest from its top right corner, since it sits at the top; the chip opens and closes on the width spring 0.32/0 with the content swap above, growing from its right edge; folding into the 36 circle beside a dictation and opening again use the same spring. The call notice enters 6 pt high at scale 0.92 to rest, spring 0.4/0.1, fading in 0.16 s, and leaves with a 0.2 s fade. The countdown's number changes in place. The PillRecord dot holds still, because a pulse pulls the eye for an hour. Tabs, folder groups and meeting steps never animate; a running step's arc turns like the getting-ready arc. With Reduce Motion each is the 0.15 s linear fade, widths jump while the content is hidden, the arc holds still; the level bars stay.

## Components
- Base: hand-rolled SwiftUI on AppKit windows, in `App/`; the shared controls live in `App/Components/`, one folder per kind: `Buttons/`, `Fields/` (text fields, switch, radio, popup, segmented control), `Labels/` (icon labels, keycap, tags, progress bar, notices), `Rows/` (form layout, model rows) and `Support/` (focus ring, floating panel, reduce motion).
- Icons: SF Symbols only, 14 pt in the window, 15 pt in the pill (centred in a 15 pt box; the check 13 pt semibold), 16 pt for permission rows, 9 pt semibold for the Details chevron. Names in use: `gearshape`, `waveform`, `text.badge.checkmark`, `text.quote`, `character.book.closed`, `rectangle.stack`, `clock.arrow.circlepath`, `magnifyingglass`, `play.circle`, `pause.circle` (the reader's play control, a speaker's lines), `doc.on.doc`, `arrow.clockwise`, `trash`, `chevron.left`, `chevron.right`, `chevron.down`, `chevron.up.chevron.down`, `checkmark`, `network`, `lock.fill`, `exclamationmark.circle`, `exclamationmark.triangle.fill`, `checkmark.circle.fill`, `mic.fill`, `mic.slash.fill`, `accessibility`, `text.alignleft`, `hourglass`, `waveform.slash`, `text.badge.xmark`, `network.slash`, `arrow.right` (a replacement's phrase to its text), `hand.raised` (Permissions), `info.circle` (About Narrari), `pencil` (edit an item or the outside service), `checkmark.circle` (make a summary template the default), `plus.square.on.square` (duplicate), `speaker.wave.2` (hear a sound set), `speaker.wave.2.fill` (the same, held still with Reduce Motion), `waveform.badge.plus` (Transcribe a file), `xmark` (dismiss a failed file, dismiss the call notice). Meetings: `record.circle` (Record a meeting, a recording meeting's list line), `square.and.arrow.down` (Import a recording), `pin` and `pin.fill` (pin a meeting; `pin.fill` 10 pt marks a pinned row), `folder` (move to a folder; 12 pt on the meta line), `stop.circle` (the page's Stop recording), `pause.fill`, `play.fill`, `stop.fill` (13 pt pill icon buttons; `pause.fill` 15 pt as the paused state), `circle.dashed` (a step waiting), `chevron.up` (previous match), 9 pt `chevron.down` and `chevron.right` on folder heads; on Permissions `speaker.wave.2` (System audio) and `calendar` (Calendar). Settings pages: `keyboard` (Shortcuts), `capsule` (Recorder), `record.circle` (Recording), `doc.text` (Summaries), `person.2` (People).
- Recorder pill: one borderless non-activating `NSPanel`, level `.statusBar`, never key, clear and click-through (except while it carries a meeting, see Meeting pill), tall enough for the capsule and two caption lines; bottom centre of the active screen, capsule bottom 18 pt above the Dock or screen edge. Panel anchored at the bottom, so nothing the caption does moves the capsule. Same look in both themes.
- Looks, chosen on Recorder as "Recorder look" (`PillSettings.look`, read live), listed glass first, then dark, then the smaller recorders: Smoked (default), Clear, Solid, Gloss, Colour, Halo, Compact, Inline. The plate always takes the capsule's look. A look is a finish (`PillFinish`: smoked, clear, solid, gloss, one file each in `App/Pill/`) and a recorder (`PillRecorder`: full, bars, words); Colour adds its hues (`App/Pill/ColourInk.swift`), Halo its light (`App/Pill/HaloRim.swift`). "Show live text" works in every look.
- Compact: Solid's finish; recording shows the level bars alone (64 wide), working shows the dots alone (44.5), notices and done as in every look; words, when on, and the no-sound notice on the plate above.
- Inline: Solid's finish; recording shows the bars and then the words in the capsule on one line (Caption face), growing to 300 for the words and never shrinking within a dictation, then the newest stay in view and the oldest fade off a 16 pt leading edge; no plate for words, only for the no-sound notice, as in Compact. With words off it shows the bars alone. After release the words go with the recording content.
- Colour row: only while Colour is the look, 8 under the Recorder look line, "Colour" with the presets at its right as swatches (`SwatchRow`, new): 18 circles, gap 8, the preset on a 0.5 pt RaisedEdge; the chosen one carries `checkmark` 10/700 in PillFill. Each is a button named by its colour; Tab reaches it, Space chooses. Presets: Blue (default), Teal, Lime, Yellow, Amber, Pink. Each is at least 4.5 on PillFill and at least 0.06 apart in OKLab from PillClean and PillDone, in both contrast modes, so cleaning up and done keep their own hues; 0.06 is what Blue keeps from PillClean under Increase Contrast.
- Preview: under the Recorder look row, 10 below, the real pill at true size on a light page (#FFFFFF) and a dark page (#1C1D21) side by side, 12 apart, 112 tall, radius 8, Rule edge; it loops `PillDemo` (listening with invented words, Cleaning up, done, a pause, 7.6 s) only while its window is on screen, and holds one listening frame with Reduce Motion.
- Smoked glass: macOS 26, one `GlassEffectContainer` (spacing 4) with the capsule and the caption plate as two `glassEffect(.regular.tint(PillFill))` shapes, tint 78% and 72%; macOS 15, an `NSVisualEffectView` (`.hudWindow`, behind window, dark) under the same tint, masked to the shape.
- Inside that glass sits a PillFill layer inside it, 88% capsule and 85% plate, and the inner rim; on macOS 15 the tint layer takes those values. Liquid Glass tint alone does not darken the glass.
- Clear: more glass than Smoked. macOS 26, `glassEffect(.clear.tint(PillFill 30%))`; macOS 15, Smoked's material. Inside it a PillFill smoke, on the capsule a vertical gradient 6% at the top, 52% at 22%, 86% from 36% to 64% where the content sits, 52% at 78%, 10% at the bottom, so the page shows through above and below the content; on the plate an even 80%, because the caption fills it; the bright rim above.
- Solid: opaque PillFill, flat inner edge, the capsule and plate shadows. Reduce Transparency gives the glass looks (Smoked, Clear, Halo) Solid's finish; the opaque looks keep theirs, and every look keeps its recorder.
- Gloss: true black (PillBlack) under a white sheen, 14% at the top to none at the middle, the 1 pt luminous edge above, the capsule and plate shadows; the edge keeps the shape over a black page.
- Colour: Solid's finish and the full recorder, with the state in one hue on its symbol, bars, dots and check: the chosen preset while listening and transcribing (sound), PillClean while cleaning up (text), PillDone for the check, PillError for errors as in every look. Words, the timer and notices stay PillText and PillMuted. Every state keeps its own symbol and words, so none rests on colour.
- Halo: Smoked's finish and the full recorder, and the level is also one line of PillText light on the capsule's top edge, centred and following the outline, 10 long in silence to 54 at full level, 1.5 wide with soft ends, brightness 55% to 100% with the level, and a glow inside the glass (7 wide at 6%, 4 wide at 16%, clipped to the capsule, because a glow outside it vanishes on a white page). Transcribing and Cleaning up keep their labels while a 40 pt segment, bright at its head, laps the real outline at constant speed. Done draws the whole outline once from the top centre, then fades, with the check. Notices and errors carry no light. The caption's newest word is full PillText and the words before it settle to 66%. Increase Contrast: opaque PillFill with a 1 pt white 50% inner edge and a 1 pt black 70% outer edge, in every look.
- Capsule: height 36, padding 14 in every state; widths come from measured labels (`PillWidths`): recording 126 (microphone 15, gap 10, meter 36, gap 10, timer box, fits 0:00 to 9:59, one digit wider from 10:00), Transcribing 151, Cleaning up 147, done 36, notices up to 400 with an ellipsis past that (the full text goes to VoiceOver and history).
- Caption: the words heard so far on their own plate 8 pt above the capsule, centred on it, only while live text is on and words have arrived; with live text off it carries no words. Padding 6.5 12, 64 to 420 wide, 29.5 tall at one line and 46 at two. Set in the Caption face (SF, 15 pt line box plus 1.5 spacing); at most two lines, the newest always in view, the oldest scrolls away upward under a 6 pt top fade; nothing is cut with an ellipsis. Live text covers only the last 14 s of audio, so each refresh is laid onto the words heard before at the longest run they share (`Caption.merge`); after release the final transcript replaces it. The caption stays through Transcribing and Cleaning up, leaves when the text lands, and is gone before any notice after release.
- No sound: when a dictation's first 2 s bring nothing from the microphone, the plate carries `waveform.slash` 12 pt, gap 6, and "No sound from" with the microphone's name in the Caption face, one line, the plate at most 420 wide with the name cut in the middle past that; the capsule records on as usual. In every look, with live text on or off. It enters like the caption and goes the moment sound arrives with a 200 ms fade, leaving the plate to the words; with Reduce Motion both are the 0.15 s linear fade. A switch of microphone in those 2 s starts the count again for the new one; silence after the first sound is a pause and shows nothing. "No sound" means every buffer under -110 dBFS mean power, far below a quiet room, so a muted, dead or wrong input and not a person yet to speak (`MicRecorder.swift`).
- A notice while recording (Downloading or Getting ready while the speech model is on its way, a mode, Clean-up on or off) opens a slot between the meter and the timer, symbol, gap 7, label; microphone and meter stay pinned to the leading edge and the timer to the trailing edge. The caption carries on above it.

## Voice
- Second person, sentence case, plain verbs. Buttons say the action ("Open Accessibility Settings", "Cancel", "Delete"). Errors say what happened, then what to do (Problems below). Pill text is a state, not a sentence: "Transcribing", "Nothing heard".
- Say only what the person needs to act: no line that repeats the title or a label, no example products or brands.

## Problems
- An error Narrari throws gives one Problem (`Sources/NarrariKit/Support/Log/Problem.swift`): a fixed code, a message, and whether Try again can help.
- The message says what happened, then what to do, in sentences with full stops: "The microphone could not start. Try again, or choose another in Settings, Speech." A pill state that is no sentence ("Nothing heard") has none.
- The code is short and never changes once shipped. Meeting files store the code, never the message, so a message can be reworded; the log prints it.
- An error without a Problem shows its feature's fixed one ("The transcript could not be written. Try again."). System text (`localizedDescription`) never reaches the screen, a file or the log.
- Problems show where they always did: the pill's notice, the meeting bar's notice, a form error on a page, a meeting step's row, a dictation's audio bar; a failed meeting also as a menu panel row and the mark on the menu bar icon. Try again shows only when the problem says it can help.
- Beside its own Try again button, the message leaves out the words that say the same (`Problem.besideRetry`): a closing "Try again." goes, and ", then try again." or " and try again." end the sentence instead ("The download failed. Check the connection."). An alternative stays ("Try again, or choose another model."). Where no button stands, in the pill, the menu panel and the `narrari` command, the whole message shows.
- Every failure is logged with `Log.failure`, its fixed name, the code and any status number, never a person's words. No empty catch.

## States
- What work is doing now is one WorkState (`Sources/NarrariKit/Support/Work/WorkState.swift`): idle, waiting with a reason, working with an activity and an optional share and start, failed with a Problem, or done. Its label is the only wording; no screen writes its own.
- Working: "Transcribing", "Cleaning up", "Writing the summary", "Finding speakers", "Checking for echo", "Importing", "Downloading, 46%", "Loading" for a list's first read, and "Getting ready" only for opening a model already on this Mac. A share reads as a percentage with a bar; without one, work with a start reads its time ("Finding speakers, 0:40"), never a made-up bar.
- Waiting: "Waits for …" ("Waits for the speech model", "Waits for the transcript", "Waits for its turn"). Failed: the problem's message (Problems above); a line too narrow for it (a list row, a panel row) says "Failed" and the message stays where the work is opened.
- `WorkLine` (`App/Components/Labels/WorkLine.swift`) draws one: the label, the bar while there is a share, the time, and Try again when the problem can help and the screen offers it. Used by the meeting steps, the file being transcribed and a list's first read; the pill, the menu panel, the meetings list, the model rows and the "again" buttons show the same labels in their own type, and the `narrari` command prints the same problem messages.
- The check refuses the retired phrases ("Writing the transcript", "Transcribing again", "Writing up the meeting", "Processing").
