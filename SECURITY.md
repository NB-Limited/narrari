# Security

Email hello@nb-limited.com with "Security" in the subject. Don't open a public issue.

## What counts

Narrari promises that your words stay on your Mac. A report that breaks that promise counts, such as:

- Audio, text or names leaving the Mac when you have not entered an outside service.
- Dictated text, meeting words or people's names written into a log.
- A service key from the Keychain reaching a file, a log or anywhere but the service it is for.

## What to expect

One maintainer reads every report and answers. There is no bug bounty.
