# Contributing

Narrari is open to read, build, fork and learn from. One person maintains it, so pull requests, issues and comments are open only to people NB Limited has invited.

## Report a bug or ask to be invited

Email hello@nb-limited.com. For a bug, say what you did, what you expected and what happened, and paste the output of `narrari doctor`.

`doctor` never shows dictated text, meeting content or service keys. It does list your microphones and speakers by name, so read it before you send it.

## Invited contributors

- An issue is a bug or an idea, one per issue; the forms ask for what is needed.
- One problem per pull request, linked to its issue.
- Say how you checked it.
- `bash scripts/ci.sh` passes.
- Read `AGENTS.md` first; its rules apply to every change.
