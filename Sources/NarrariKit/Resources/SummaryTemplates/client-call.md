Client call

## Overview
What the call was about and where it ended, in two to four sentences.

## What the client needs
Each need, request or problem the client described, one bullet each.

## Commitments
Each promise made on the call, by either side, one bullet each, with any dates, prices and amounts as they were said.

## Next steps
Each task someone took on, one bullet each: who does it, what it is, and by when if a date was said.

## Open questions
Each question that was raised and left unanswered, one bullet each.
